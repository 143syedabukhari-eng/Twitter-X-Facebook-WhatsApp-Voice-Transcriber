Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 15, 2024

This Privacy Policy explains how Twitter X, Facebook & WhatsApp Voice Transcriber handles user data. Our extension is designed with a "Privacy-First" approach.

1. Data Collection & Usage

The Extension handles the following types of data to provide its core service:

  • Audio Data: When you click the "Transcribe" button, the extension intercepts the audio stream of that specific voice message. This data is processed in real-time. We use high-accuracy AI models to transcribe this audio. The audio data is processed transiently and is never stored or archived by the developer.
  • Personal Communications: The extension accesses the audio of the voice message to provide a text transcription. This content is processed and displayed to you within your browser session and is not collected or stored on our servers.
  • Authentication Information: If you choose to use your own API keys (Google Gemini, OpenAI, AssemblyAI, or Deepgram), they are stored locally on your device using chrome.storage.sync and are only used to authenticate requests to the respective AI providers.

2. Third-Party Data Sharing

To provide transcription and translation, data is securely transmitted to the following providers based on your settings:

  • Google Gemini (via our Vercel Proxy): Audio data is sent for transcription when using the free tier.
  • AssemblyAI, OpenAI, or Deepgram: Audio data is sent for transcription if you use your own API key.
  • Google Translate: Transcribed text is sent for translation if the feature is enabled.

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to any third parties.

3. Permissions Justification

  • Storage: Used to save your API keys and language preferences locally.
  • Host Permissions (WhatsApp, Facebook, X): Necessary to detect voice messages and inject the "Transcribe" button into the chat interface across supported platforms.

4. User Rights

You can delete all data stored by the extension at any time by clearing the extension settings or uninstalling the extension.
